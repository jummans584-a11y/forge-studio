👋 Welcome to Forge Studio

A quick tour before you dive in — this only shows once.
🧍⛰Two workspacesSwitch between Character (paint/sculpt a model) and Map/Terrain (build a scene) up top.
🧰Tools live in the bottom panelDrag the handle at the top of the panel up or down to resize it. Tabs inside switch between Sculpt, Object, Paint, Vegetation, Export and more.
📚Asset LibrarySave models you import so you can reuse and scatter them later without re-importing.
↶Undo is always thereWorks across every tool — sculpt, paint, objects, vegetation — so it's safe to experiment.
⚠️Watch the poly countMobile has limited memory. If a warning about a "heavy scene" shows up, try Optimize → Merge before it slows down.
